Thom "Spebby" Mott is a California-based graduate student affiliated with [SLOTHlab](https://slothlab.info/about/index.html) and part time developer working in interactive digital media, specialising in Game AI and Computer Graphics. As an undergraduate, he was affiliated with the [SET Lab](https://setlab.soe.ucsc.edu/) and SLOTHlab, contributing to projects including "Compassionate AR" and "Procedural Content Generation in Minecraft." Alongside his personal work, these experiences have shaped an approach that blends technical practice with human-centred design.

Outside academia, Thom has been involved in game development for over a decade, getting started with video game modding in the 2010s. He released multiple officially endorsed curated maps for [Unturned](https://store.steampowered.com/app/304930/Unturned). He has also published several collaborative projects on [Newgrounds](https://spebby.newgrounds.com/). He is currently working on the upcoming action-roguelike [Gamba Gun](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3497930/Gamba_Gun).
